1. Introduction

Welcome to MINDORA ("we," "our," or "us"). MINDORA is a strategy and brain-training mobile application featuring Online and Offline Chess, 2D Ludo, and brain-training puzzles such as Sudoku, Memory Challenge, Math Quiz, Word Puzzle, Reaction Speed, Pattern Recognition, Logic Puzzles, and Number Sequence.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and protect it, and your rights regarding your data when you use the MINDORA Android application.

2. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide (Google Sign-In and Player Profile)

You can use available guest/offline features or sign in using Google Sign-In through Android Credential Manager to access supported online features. When you sign in or customize your profile, we may collect and store:

  • Account identifiers: Your Firebase Authentication user ID (UID) and an automatically generated in-app Player ID (for example, MND-XXXXXX).
  • Profile information: Your chosen in-app username/display name and selected in-app illustrated avatar ID.
  • Social and privacy preferences: Friend connections, friend requests, and preferences such as allowing friend requests or showing online status.

B. Gameplay and Virtual Progress Data

To provide matchmaking, leaderboards, match history, and progression across supported devices, we may store:

  • Game statistics and ratings, including Chess ELO, 2D Ludo statistics, wins and losses, player level, XP, achievements, and challenge progress.
  • Online match records, including Chess board states (FEN), Ludo token positions, dice-roll records, move history, and match results.
  • Virtual Gold Coin balance and transaction history, including coins earned through gameplay, welcome bonuses, achievements, and match prize pools.

C. Locally Stored Data

Offline gameplay records and app settings—such as sound, music, board theme, piece style, and notification preferences—may be stored on your device using local app storage. Guest progress synchronization after account linking depends on the features implemented in the app. Guest data stored only on the device may be lost if app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

3. Virtual Currency Policy (No Real-Money Gambling)

MINDORA includes a virtual points system called Gold Coins.

  • Virtual use: Gold Coins are virtual entertainment points used within the app.
  • No monetary value: Gold Coins have no real-world monetary value and cannot be redeemed, withdrawn, or exchanged for money, goods, or services.
  • No cash-out: Gold Coins are not intended to function as real-money deposits, withdrawals, or cash prizes.

4. Permissions We Use

MINDORA may request the Android permissions needed for its network features:

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to connect to authentication and backend services for supported online gameplay, leaderboards, and profile synchronization.

The app's actual permissions are those declared in its published Android manifest. Do not rely on this list as a substitute for checking the final app build.

5. How We Use Your Information

We use information collected through the app to:

  • Authenticate accounts and preserve supported game progress.
  • Match players in supported online games, friend matches, and private rooms.
  • Display leaderboards, player profiles, and match history.
  • Support fair play, detect suspicious activity, and maintain ratings, levels, achievements, and virtual Gold Coin records.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve app functionality and security.

We do not sell users' personal data. Data may be processed by service providers that help operate the app, as described below.

6. Third-Party Services

MINDORA uses Google/Firebase services for supported backend features. These may include:

  • Google Sign-In / Android Credential Manager: For supported account sign-in.
  • Firebase Authentication: To manage authenticated user sessions.
  • Cloud Firestore: To store supported player profiles, online match states, friend records, and leaderboard data.

These services process information under their own terms and privacy documentation. Please review the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security information.

7. Data Security

We aim to protect information using appropriate technical and organizational safeguards. Network connections to supported backend services should use encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS), and backend access should be controlled through properly configured authentication and database security rules. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and security depends on the final deployed configuration.

8. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain online account information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain records, resolve disputes, and meet applicable legal obligations.

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data by emailing wedeveloper33@gmail.com. Include your Player ID and, if applicable, the email address associated with your sign-in so that we can locate the account. We may ask for reasonable verification before processing a request.

Local guest data can generally be removed by clearing MINDORA's app storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. This does not necessarily delete data already stored on our servers.
